## W1 pattern summary

**Two-pointer works when:** the search space has a monotone property that makes pointer-move outcomes predictable — you can eliminate positions without risking valid solutions.

**Three ways to establish it:**
1. **Sort first** (LC#15) — sorting imposes a global monotone order.
2. **Problem math gives it for free** (LC#11) — the area formula guarantees moving the taller pointer is never optimal.
3. **Structural symmetry** (LC#125) — palindrome definition means converging from both ends is always valid.
